How it works / booking logic
Reproduces the reference Tally export exactly (Composition dealer → no output GST). Per order (invoice = 25-26/REC-####):
1 · Sale (invoice) — Dr SUNDRY DEBTORS RESTAURENT (New Ref) / Cr SALES UNDER COMPOSITION SCHEME.
2 · Journal (mode routing) — Dr channel debtor (New Ref) / Cr SUNDRY DEBTORS RESTAURENT (Agst Ref). Mode is derived (EZee has none): order matched to a Paytm settlement txn → CARD/UPI/PAYTM [F&B], else cash → SANDEEP SHARMA IMP A/C.
3 · Receipt — one per bank credit: Dr Indian Bank / Cr channel debtor. 4 · Payment — one per bank debit: Dr <auto-mapped ledger> / Cr Indian Bank.
Swiggy & Zomato (sales, receipts, and the RCM commission Purchase entries) are left for the accountant.
