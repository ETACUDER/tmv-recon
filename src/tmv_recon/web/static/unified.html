🏦 Ledger Hierarchy (TMV Setup)

Revenue Ledgers
  • SALE ACCOMODATION GST @ 5 % (note: space before %)
  • SALE ACCOMODATION GST @ 12%
  • SALE ACCOMODATION GST @ 18%
  • CGST (output tax)
  • SGST (output tax)
Asset/Receivables
  • Sundry Debtors (All OTAs + guests)
  • CARD / UPI / PAYTM / G PAY (combined payment modes)
  • CARD / UPI / PAYTM / G PAY [F&B] (F&B payments)
  • INDIAN BANK CARD (bank-specific)
Current Setup (FY25-26): Guest bookings → "Sundry Debtors" ledger
• Individual tracking via Invoice# in narration (e.g., "INVOICE NO:-25-26/6453 MRS. MADHUR PIPARSANIA")
• Note: "AGODA COMPANY PTE LTD" exists under Sundry Creditors (commission payable), not used for booking revenue
• Payment settlements through "CARD / UPI / PAYTM / G PAY" combined ledger

⚙️ 3-Stage Reconciliation Approach

STAGE 1: EXACT
Match on:
• invoice_no
• UTR (bank ref)
Confidence: 1.0
STAGE 2: FUZZY
Match on:
• Guest name (≥70%)
• Amount (±1%)
• Date window (±7d)
STAGE 3: MANUAL
Unmatched queue with:
• Reason codes
• Manual review
• Data quality fixes

  • ✓ Sign convention verified against 8,628 sales vouchers

⚠️ Tally Sign Convention (Critical!)

ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE flag controls Dr/Cr:
• Dr (Debit): ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE=Yes + negative amount (Assets, Expenses, Debtors)
• Cr (Credit): ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE=No + positive amount (Revenue, Liabilities, GST)
Reverse this = accounting chaos!
✅ Verified against FY25-26 actual vouchers (3,133 Sales + 22 Journal)
